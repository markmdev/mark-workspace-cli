Privacy policy

Mark Workspace CLI is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, Mark, and it works only with his own Google accounts.

What it accesses

With his permission, it lists, reads and uploads files in his own Google Drive, and reads his own Gmail. It does not send, change or delete email.

How it uses and stores data

It runs only on his own computer. What it reads goes only to that computer. It stores nothing on any server, and its sign-in is kept encrypted on that computer.

Sharing

It shares nothing with anyone, sells nothing, and shows no ads.

Google user data

Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's Third-party connections page.
